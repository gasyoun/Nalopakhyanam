**Status: name-reservation** (24-09-2026) — this repository reserves the name; no active content yet. See [consolidation study §4e](https://github.com/gasyoun/Uprava/blob/main/CONSOLIDATION_SPIKE_REPOLESS_DICTIONARIES_THIN_VIEW_REPOS_2026Q3.md).

A small text-study workspace for the **Nalopākhyāna** (नलोपाख्यान) — the Nala
and Damayantī episode of the *Āraṇyaka-parvan* (Book of the Forest) of the
*Mahābhārata*. Content here is in **Russian**.

## Contents

- [`editions-numbering.md`](https://github.com/gasyoun/Nalopakhyanam/blob/main/editions-numbering.md)
  — a comparative table of chapter numbering across editions of the
  *Mahābhārata* (Bombay/Vulgate, Critical Edition, Calcutta, Kumbhakonam) for
  the episode «Встреча Дамаянти с караваном» (Damayantī's meeting with the
  caravan), with notes on Nīlakaṇṭha's commentary (*Bhārata-bhāva-dīpa*) and
  how the Bombay/Chitrashala text relates to the Poona Critical Edition.
